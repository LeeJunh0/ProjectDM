ONE square top-down 3x3 grass terrain rule tile atlas with original arrangement: broad connected grassy center, irregular grassy perimeter, thin warm brown soil edge, dark brown outside. Preserve muted olive green/yellow-green palette and warm earth colors. Dramatically simplify fine grass speckles: center should be 80 percent quiet low-contrast flat green areas, with occasional irregular chunky patches of shade and a few sparse 2-3 blade grass tufts, not a carpet of dots. Keep center darker and quieter than the original so player/monster sprites read well. No new flowers, trees, rocks or props. Orthographic flat top-down, not a raised isometric island. No black outline around everything, no shiny rims, no lighting glow, no text, no visible grid lines, no labels, no characters.
