# 실제 사용 32×32 픽셀 타일 바닥

내장 image_gen으로 돌판 줄눈이 있는 외곽/내부 그림을 생성하고 nearest-neighbor로 외곽 96×96(3×3), 내부 128×128(4×4) PNG로 변환했다. 각 실제 스프라이트는 32×32, Point 필터, PPU 32다. 내부는 16개 타일을 좌표에 맞춰 이어 쓰며 개별 타일 회전은 사용하지 않는다. 줄눈이 이어지는 큰 패턴으로 한 칸의 반복을 줄인다. 초원에는 영향을 주지 않는다.

## 내부 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: production-style 2D PIXEL GAME TILESET interior stone paving, ONE square seamless repeat texture to be sliced into 4 columns x 4 rows of 32x32 pixel game tiles (128x128 LOGICAL PIXELS TOTAL). Render as exact crisp pixel-art enlarged with uniform square pixels, every logical pixel visible and hard edged. Flat orthographic TOP-DOWN dungeon paving, NO perspective, NO realistic texture. A connected floor of hand-laid dark desaturated blue-violet rectangular flagstone tiles in staggered courses, varied slab widths and heights, thin ONE logical-pixel muted dark grout lines, only 3-4 flat shades per slab. Approximately 8 uneven staggered stone courses across whole 128px sheet, long stones 18-36 logical pixels wide, short stones 8-14 pixels wide. Grout and stones continue seamlessly across opposite edges of the full sheet. DO NOT draw 4x4 cell borders: logical game tile cut boundaries are INVISIBLE and NOT the masonry grout. Paving joints and slab faces cross those cut boundaries naturally. Keep faces calm and mostly flat; occasional 1-2 pixel chipped corners or tiny cracks, no scratch stamps and no bright highlight triangle repeated in every stone. Use at most 10 colors, mean stone #30354f to #3b405c, darker grout #24283e. Contrast restrained but joints readable. Low detail clean hand-placed pixels, no anti-aliasing, NO soft gradients, NO brush strokes, NO smooth shading, NO high-resolution painterly details, no fine grain, no noise, no directional light, no bevels. Fully opaque square filled edge to edge, NO perimeter rocks, NO outer border, no transparency, no moss, no text, no guide lines, no objects. This is a retro pixel-game terrain tile texture, not an illustration.

## 외곽 생성 프롬프트

Use case: style-transfer. Redraw the attached complete 3x3 dungeon ground rule tile atlas as a FLOOR BUILT FROM LAID STONE PAVING SLABS, not bare natural bedrock. Same dark desaturated blue-purple palette and coarse pixel-art style. Entire square is one cohesive connected paved floor, made of medium-to-large rectangular and slightly irregular polygonal flagstones with narrow muted dark grout joints. Stagger the joints, vary stone widths and occasional chipped corners, avoid a perfectly uniform checkerboard, avoid diagonal scratch stamp motifs. About 6-8 irregular courses across the full atlas; most stone faces quiet with 2-3 subtle flat shade bands, no bright beveled corner triangle on every stone. Same paving material, joint width and stone scale through all nine regions. All inner cut lines of the 3x3 atlas must be invisible game tile boundaries: masonry joints are staggered and must connect across tile boundaries, not outline the nine large squares. Central third is a seamless horizontally and vertically tileable masonry motif with matching grout positions at opposite edges, designed for repeated layout without directional rotation. Border eight regions continue the exact same laid flagstone paving to the outside, where only the final row of stones is broken or chipped irregularly. Outer edge is the edge of this paved floor, not a separate ring of rocks. Tiny sparse muted moss ONLY in a few exterior joints. Exterior gaps beyond the broken stone perimeter truly transparent. Flat top-down orthographic, low-contrast restrained ambient light, handcrafted chunky stepped pixel clusters approximately 32 logical pixels per game tile, 96 logical pixels across whole atlas. No fine noise, no soft painting, no bare smooth empty center, no continuous bright border, no text, no grid guides, no characters or props.
