# 외곽과 내부를 하나의 암반으로 통합

내장 image_gen으로 석재 3×3 아틀라스 전체를 다시 생성했다. 중앙 단독 교체 대신 GroundRule_1_1도 같은 아틀라스로 되돌렸다. 외곽은 같은 재질 암반의 부서진 끝으로 표현한다. 던전 룰에만 varyInteriorRotation을 활성화해 중앙 무늬를 좌표 기반으로 0/90/180/270도 회전한다. 실행 중 난수나 초원·외곽의 방향에는 영향을 주지 않는다.

## 생성 프롬프트

Use case: style-transfer. Redesign this complete 3x3 top-down pixel-art dungeon terrain atlas as ONE unified continuous bedrock landmass. Critical: the eight perimeter tiles and central tile share EXACTLY the SAME base stone material, base color #30354f, shading range and pixel scale at EVERY internal join. Outer rim must feel like the broken edge OF this same flat bedrock, never a separate ring of decorative stones surrounding a different surface. Keep the overall original 3x3 square layout with nine equal regions but draw NO dividing grid, NO square corner bevels, NO diagonal scratch motifs. The center tile is a quiet seamless repeating base: 90% uniform dark blue-violet stone, just 3 small subtle irregular pixel mottles within its interior, with equal uniform stone color along all four of its edges, no directional lighting. Keep that exact same quiet stone texture continuing through inner portions of all eight border tiles. Only the OUTERMOST 15% of the complete atlas has irregular crumbled rock silhouettes, a few thin cracks and very sparse muted moss near exterior. At the rim use only modest highlights on a few broken outward edges; no continuous bright line, no black moat, no cobblestone necklace. Avoid light patches at the joins between any tiles. Coarse hand-placed pixel clusters, roughly 32 logical pixels per tile, about 96x96 logical pixels across the whole atlas enlarged crisply; muted 5-shade stone palette, no smooth gradients, no fine surface grain or dense noise. Whole continuous floor stays fully opaque. Exterior negative spaces around the irregular outer silhouette are genuinely transparent, as in the reference. No characters, no text, no frame, no checkerboard painted as background. This must work both as one small 3x3 island AND expanded with its center repeated many times without visibly separate square slabs.
